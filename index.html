HOW TO PLAY

The object: trap your opponent's knight (L) so it has no legal move on its turn. SUGGESTION: start a game, attempt a move or two, select change mode button to return to HOW TO PLAY, to gain a better understanding.


The center square may never be occupied by either player.


Each turn consists of two moves:
(1) Move your knight, then
(2) Move any one of your four pawns.


A knight's move is legal when it occupies at least one new square from its current position. It may be slid, rotated, flipped, or placed elsewhere — as long as it occupies at least one new square.


A knight is identified by its head "H" and foot "F". When placing the head, if multiple foot positions are possible, a second selection will be required to place the foot.


A pawn may move to any vacant square. The UNDO button is to undo the knight move. To undo a highlighted pawn selection, select it again.


Players alternate until one player cannot move their knight. The player who made the last full move wins.


Hint: After moving your knight, move a pawn that is adjacent to it — don't let your own pawns block your future knight moves.
